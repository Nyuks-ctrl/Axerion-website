PROJECT OVERVIEW

Rhandzus Decor is a professional event planning and decor website designed to showcase the design and development skills of Axerion. The project involved creating a visually appealing and user-friendly website that highlights the services offered by Rhandzus Decor, including premium catering, event planning for weddings, kiddies parties, baby showers, and more. The design incorporates a modern aesthetic with a focus on clean lines, vibrant colors, and engaging imagery to attract potential clients and enhance the overall user experience.
